ABOUT US

We help tuition centres in Hyderabad keep monthly fees on track.
Our simple fee follow-up board shows who is due, who has promised to pay, and what is still pending.
Automatic reminders help your team follow up on time, without losing track of a conversation.

YOUR QUESTIONS

1. What exactly do you set up?
We build a simple board for tracking fee due dates, payments promised, and pending amounts, plus automatic fee reminders.

2. Who is this for?
It is for tuition centres that need a clearer way to follow up when parents pay monthly fees late.

3. What does the fee board show?
It gives your team a simple view of who is due, who has promised to pay, and what still needs follow-up.

4. Can we record when a parent promises to pay?
Yes. Your team can mark a promise and its expected date so it is easy to see what to follow up on.

5. Do the reminders collect fees automatically?
No. The reminders prompt parents to pay. Your centre still receives and records the payment through its usual method.

6. Can we choose when reminders go out?
We will agree on a practical reminder timing with you during setup and configure it for your follow-up process.

7. Will this stop every late payment?
We cannot promise that. It helps your team spot dues and follow up consistently, but parents still choose when to pay.

8. Do we need technical skills to use the board?
No special technical skills are needed. We will show your team how to update and read the board.

9. Can the board fit our fee dates and batches?
We will set it up around your centre's fee cycle and basic batch details. We will confirm any special requirements before starting.

10. Can more than one staff member use it?
The demo setup is intended for your team to use together. We will agree who needs access and how to manage it before setup.

11. How long does setup take?
Our demo estimate is about 5 to 7 business days after we receive the agreed student and fee details.

12. What information do you need from us?
We will ask for the fee schedule, the student or parent details needed for follow-up, and your preferred reminder wording and timing.

13. How do we get started?
Call or email us during our demo business hours. We will learn how you track fees now, confirm what you need, and agree on the setup.

14. How much does setup cost?
Setup is Rs 15,000. It covers building and configuring your fee follow-up board and automatic reminder for the agreed process.

15. What is the monthly price?
The monthly fee is Rs 5,000. It covers keeping the agreed reminder and board service available, plus routine help with it.

16. Are there any other charges?
Our stated price is Rs 15,000 setup plus Rs 5,000 a month. Any third-party messaging or tax charges, if applicable, will be discussed before you commit.

17. When does the monthly fee begin?
For this demo offer, we assume the monthly fee begins once setup is complete. We will confirm the billing date with you in writing before work starts.

18. What happens after we pay?
We confirm the payment, collect the agreed setup details, configure your board and reminders, and arrange a short team walkthrough before handover.

19. What support do we get after launch?
The demo monthly plan includes routine help with the agreed board and reminders during our business hours. Larger changes can be discussed separately.
